# 6. Monitoring, scheduled results, recovery and practice

[Testing home](README.md) · Next: [Platform owner](07-platform-owner.md)

Use the completed/interrupted sittings from the previous steps. Keep administrator and student sessions separate. Use a disposable attempt for suspension and a different one for recovery.

1. **Live monitoring.** Open Live exams and the test sitting. Expect the correct roster, attempt state, readiness/contact information and visible refresh behavior. Stale contact or missing telemetry must not be presented as proof the student is offline or cheating.
2. **Grouped history.** Open a student's activity history after generating interruption events. Expect four groups: window/tab changes, screen protection, watermark integrity and returns. Check counts/latest received time and expand original records. Advanced analysis should use factual event-type colors and counts, not a misconduct score or automatic penalty recommendation.
3. **Exports.** Export the available incident/history report. Compare its scope and timestamps to the UI; distinguish the latest/current-page records from a complete historical export. Repeated or delayed events must not invent duplicate incidents. No password, MFA secret or answer key should appear in supervision exports.
4. **Suspend/resume.** Explicitly suspend a disposable attempt with a reason. After the student receives the server decision, editing must pause. Reload cannot bypass suspension. Explicit administrator resume allows online revalidation with the original deadline. An offline browser cannot receive a new decision instantly. Retain pending answers for review.
5. **Disciplinary actions.** Where offered, test exclusion/disqualification only on a separate synthetic attempt with explicit reason/confirmation. Verify audit history and correct result eligibility. No focus event should automatically deduct marks or disqualify a student.
6. **Before results release.** Open student Official results before the scheduled time. Expect no early marks/ranks. As administrator, edit the release date to a valid later future time; refresh and verify it persists. A stale edit from a second tab must conflict.
7. **Automatic release.** Keep student results open through the release time or reopen afterward. Expect results on the next authorized refresh, without a manual Publish action, once all submission acceptance windows close. Extra-time students can delay this gate. This is request-driven release, not an email/SMS notification service.
8. **Release cutoff.** Once the existing release time arrives, rescheduling must be blocked. Already released results cannot be hidden again. Repeat result reads: expect stable scores and no duplicate publication revision. Verify known synthetic answer keys produce the expected score/pass status and visible report values.
9. **Recovery pending.** Use the documented pending-upload/recovery flow for an interrupted attempt. At release, unresolved recovery must remain pending with no fabricated zero/rank. Inspect the evidence and accept or reject with a reason. Unauthorized staff or another school must not decide it.
10. **Accepted correction.** After accepting valid recovery, expect the corrected result to appear automatically when release gates allow it. The older published snapshot/report must remain unchanged. Repeating the decision must not duplicate receipts or revisions. Rejected evidence must remain retained and auditable.
11. **Practice.** Before release or when not enabled, practice must stay unavailable. Enable it through the authorized school workflow after all related sitting windows close. Expect approved explanations and answer feedback, repeat practice as supported, and no alteration of official marks. Postponing results before cutoff must also delay practice availability.
12. **Role access and reports.** Students see their own results; teachers see only authorized results; administrators stay within their school. Compare downloadable/printable reports to the UI. Reports should remain printable outside the protected runner. Check phone layout and any existing analytics/filters with known small fixtures rather than assuming every proposed chart exists.

Historical manual-publication instructions are superseded by scheduled automatic release.
